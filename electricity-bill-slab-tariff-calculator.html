Enter your units and see the exact slab-by-slab bill math for MEPCO, LESCO, FESCO — with editable rates for any DISCO.

Quick answer

Pakistan's residential electricity bill uses a slab system: the first 100 units are charged the cheapest rate, the next 100 at a higher rate, and so on — NOT your total units at one rate. That is why 350 units can cost far more than 3.5× the cost of 100 units. This calculator shows each slab's exact share, adds duty, GST and fixed charges, and comes with MEPCO, LESCO and FESCO presets.

How the electricity bill slab tariff calculator works

Bill = Σ (units in each slab × slab rate) + electricity duty% + GST% + fixed charges  ·  Slabs: 1–100, 101–200, 201–300, 301–700, 701+

The single most common misunderstanding is the slab (tehtar) system: rates apply per slab, cumulatively. If you consume 350 units, the first 100 are billed at the cheapest slab, units 101–200 at the second, 201–300 at the third, and only units 301–350 at the fourth. Crossing a slab boundary raises only the marginal units' rate.

Beyond energy charges, a typical bill adds:

  • Electricity duty — about 1.5% on domestic consumers
  • GST — 17% on the energy cost
  • FPA (fuel price adjustment) — the big variable: set monthly by NEPRA, it can swing a bill by thousands of rupees. This tool deliberately leaves it out and says so, rather than pretending to know it.
  • Meter rent, TV fee, bank charges, surcharges — small fixed items

Default rates are published residential (non-protected) rates for MEPCO, LESCO and FESCO as of October 2026 — every rate is editable, so match your own bill and the math is exact for you.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my bill higher than units × one rate?

Because Pakistan uses cumulative slabs: each slab of units is billed at its own rate, and once you cross a slab (e.g. 200→201 units) the marginal units cost more. There are also protected vs non-protected categories: protected consumers (with low recent usage) pay less on the same units.

What is FPA and why isn't it included?

FPA (Fuel Price Adjustment) is a per-unit surcharge NEPRA revises every month based on generation fuel costs. It can differ wildly between bills, so an honest calculator cannot predict it — this tool shows your slab-based energy charges plus standard taxes, and tells you to check FPA on your actual bill.

Which DISCO rates are built in?

Residential non-protected presets for MEPCO (3.95/8.27/13.27/16.62/19.26), LESCO (4.16/8.48/13.48/16.83/19.47) and FESCO (4.03/8.35/13.35/16.70/19.34) PKR/kWh across the five slabs, as of October 2026. Every rate is editable — set exact values from your latest bill for a precise estimate.
